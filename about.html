📋 How Citizen Applications Work

1

Citizen Prepares at Home

Online - Citizen

What citizens do:

  • 📱 Browse available services on SarkariSathi
  • 📺 Watch video guides and tutorials
  • 📄 Check required documents list
  • 💰 Check fees and processing time
  • 📅 Book queue/appointment for visit
↓
2

Visit Office with Documents

In-Person - Citizen

Citizen arrives at office:

  • 🏢 Go to government office on booked date/time
  • ✅ Check-in at reception with queue number
  • 📋 Bring all required documents (original + copies)
  • ⏰ Wait for your queue number to be called
↓
3

Officer Creates Application

At Counter - Officer

Officer processes:

  • 👤 Verify citizen identity and documents
  • 💻 Create application in system
  • 🎫 Generate tracking number (e.g., PAS-2025-00123)
  • 📸 Collect biometric data if needed
  • 💳 Collect payment/fees
  • 🧾 Print receipt with tracking number for citizen
↓
4

Track Application Online

Online - Citizen

Citizen monitors progress:

  • 🔍 Enter tracking number on website
  • 📊 See real-time status updates
  • 🔔 Receive notifications on status changes
  • 💬 Message officer if questions
  • 📅 Book pickup appointment when ready
↓
5

Officer Updates Status

Backend - Officer

Application stages:

  • ✅ Submitted → Document Verification
  • 🔄 Biometric Processing
  • 🔍 External Verification (Police, etc.)
  • 🖨️ Printing
  • ✨ Ready for Pickup
  • 🎉 Completed
↓
6

Citizen Collects Document

In-Person - Citizen

Final step:

  • 🔔 Receive notification: "Ready for Pickup"
  • 📅 Book pickup queue slot
  • 🏢 Visit office with tracking receipt
  • ✅ Verify identity
  • 📄 Collect document
  • ✍️ Sign acknowledgment

What Citizens Can Do Online vs Offline

✅ Online Features (SarkariSathi Website)

  • Browse all government services
  • Watch video guides and tutorials
  • Check document requirements
  • Book queue/appointment slots
  • Track application status in real-time
  • View queue position live
  • Message officers directly
  • File complaints
  • View notifications
  • Manage profile

🏢 Must Visit Office For

  • Submit physical documents
  • Initial application creation
  • Biometric data collection (fingerprint, photo)
  • Payment of fees
  • Document verification by officer
  • Signature/authentication
  • Final document pickup

💡 Why This Hybrid Approach?

Security & Authenticity: Government documents like passports, citizenship certificates, and licenses require physical verification, biometric data, and secure handling. Officers must verify original documents and identity in person.

Legal Requirements: Many government processes require face-to-face verification and physical signatures as per Nepal's legal framework.

Digital Convenience: While submission is offline, the platform saves citizens time by letting them prepare, book slots, and track progress online - eliminating multiple visits and long waits.
